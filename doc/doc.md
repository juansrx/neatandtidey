<h1>Neat and Tidey</h1>
<h3>By juansrx</h3>

<article>
  <h2>Description</h2>
  
  <hr>
  
  <p>
  What awaits beyond the known waters?, It's time to sail away and face the most unruly waves.
  </p>
  
  <p>
  Guide your ship through the most dangerous obstacles, including natural hazards and 
  wild creatures.
  </p>
  
  <p>
  Your patience, dextery and perception are key to arrive to your destination.
  </p>
</article>

<article>
  <h2>Instructions</h2>
  
  <hr>
  
  <p>
  Point and click over the position you want to move.
  </p>
  
  <p>
  The ship will sail towards this position and it will stop only when it reaches it.
  </p>
  
  <p>
  Watch out for rocks, creatures, and other hazards. (Rule of thumb: If it isn't water, is dangerous)
  </p>
  
  <p>
  Generally, you must reach the open space at the bottom of the screen to reach the next level.
  </p>
  
  <p>
  Some levels have additional surprises awaiting, so be careful and patient.
  </p>
  
  
</article>
